Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 29, 2026 · Operated by Salzventure Technologies LLC

StarSol is operated by Salzventure Technologies LLC. StarSol provides astronomy media, constellation learning, sky guidance, and subscription features for TV and web. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, how we use and share it, and how to contact us.

Information We Collect

  • Account information such as email address, display name, authentication provider, and subscription plan.
  • Device and pairing information needed to connect a TV app to your account.
  • Approximate observing location if you grant location access or save a location, used for sky conditions, ISS pass planning, and aurora context.
  • Precise latitude and longitude only if you grant browser or device location access. You can instead use a default or manually saved location.
  • Usage information such as app screens, playback state, feature interactions, and error logs used to operate and improve the service.
  • Limited billing metadata from Stripe for web purchases and Google Play subscription status for Google TV purchases. We do not receive or store raw payment-card numbers.
  • Communications you send to us, including support requests, privacy requests, and subscription questions.

How We Use Information

  • To provide account access, subscriptions, restore purchases, and TV pairing.
  • To show location-aware astronomy guidance when you enable those features.
  • To maintain security, debug failures, prevent abuse, and improve product quality.
  • To send transactional messages such as account, password, billing, and service notices.
  • To comply with legal, tax, accounting, payment-platform, and app-store obligations.

Location Data

Location is used only for astronomy features such as sky context, ISS pass planning, weather/cloud context, and aurora watch status. If you grant location access, your device or browser may provide precise latitude and longitude. We use that location to request astronomy, weather, and map context and to save your chosen observing location if you ask us to. You can deny or revoke location permission in your browser or device settings; StarSol will use a default or saved location until you change it.

Payments

Web payments are processed by Stripe. Google TV subscriptions are processed by Google Play Billing. Each payment provider handles payment data under its own terms and privacy policy. StarSol receives only the information needed to verify entitlement, manage access, troubleshoot billing, and maintain required records. Deleting your StarSol account does not automatically cancel a subscription managed by Google Play or another app store; you must cancel those subscriptions through the store account where you purchased them.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information with service providers that help operate StarSol, including hosting, email, authentication, analytics, billing, and astronomy/weather data services. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect users, StarSol, or the public.

Current service providers and data flows include hosting and database providers for account operation, Google and Apple for optional sign-in, Cloudflare Turnstile for signup abuse prevention, Stripe for web payments, Google Play Billing for Google TV purchases, Resend for transactional email, Open-Meteo for weather context, OpenStreetMap/Nominatim for optional reverse geocoding, WhereTheISS.at for ISS position context, and NASA/ESA/STScI/ESO/NOIRLab media sources or CDNs for astronomy imagery and video. Some third-party media, font, or data requests may receive your IP address and user-agent as part of normal internet delivery.

Retention

We keep account and subscription records while your account is active and for as long afterward as reasonably needed for legal, security, accounting, tax, dispute, and operational purposes. Billing and transaction records may be retained for up to seven years where needed for accounting, tax, or payment-platform compliance. Diagnostic, security, and server logs are generally retained for a limited period, commonly 30 to 90 days, unless needed longer to investigate abuse, fraud, payment issues, or security events.

Your Choices

  • You can cancel subscriptions through the platform where you subscribed. Google Play subscriptions must be cancelled through Google Play.
  • You can disable location permission in your device or browser settings.
  • You can request account deletion, data access, correction, or export through available in-app tools or by emailing hello@starsol.app.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to know, access, correct, delete, portability, restriction, objection, or appeal. We will honor legally required requests after verifying your identity.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information. No internet service is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee that unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or interruption will never occur.

International Use

StarSol is operated from the United States. If you use the Service from outside the United States, your information may be processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate, subject to applicable law.

Children

StarSol is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

  • Right to Know. You may request information about the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you, the categories of sources, the business or commercial purposes for collection, and the categories of third parties with whom we share it.
  • Right to Delete. You may request deletion of personal information we have collected from you, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to Correct. You may request correction of inaccurate personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale/Sharing. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. No opt-out is required, but you may contact us to confirm.
  • Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information. If you provide precise geolocation data for sky and ISS pass features, you may request that we limit its use to only providing the requested service. Contact privacy@salzventure.com or use the location controls in Settings.

Non-Discrimination. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your California privacy rights. We will not deny you goods or services, charge different prices, or provide a different level of service based on your exercise of these rights.

Authorized Agents. California residents may designate an authorized agent to make a request on their behalf. Provide written authorization and contact privacy@salzventure.com. We may verify your identity directly.

Shine the Light. We do not disclose personal information to third parties for their direct marketing purposes.

Response Time. We will respond to verifiable requests within 45 days. We may extend the response period by one additional 45-day period when necessary, with notice.

To submit a California privacy request, contact: privacy@salzventure.com.

Other U.S. State Privacy Rights

Residents of Virginia (VCDPA), Colorado (CPA), Connecticut (CTDPA), Texas (TDPSA), Utah (UCPA), and other states with applicable privacy laws have rights similar to those described above, including rights to access, delete, correct, and opt out of certain processing. European Economic Area and UK Users (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom, the following applies to your use of StarSol:

Legal Bases for Processing. We process your personal data on the following legal bases: (a) performance of a contract — to provide your subscription and account; (b) legitimate interests — to maintain security, improve the service, and prevent fraud; (c) consent — where you optionally enable location features or usage analytics (you may withdraw consent at any time); and (d) legal obligation — to retain billing and tax records as required by law.

International Transfers. Transfers of personal data outside the EEA or UK, including to our U.S.-based service providers, rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission and, where applicable, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement.

Your Rights. You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing of, and receive a portable copy of your personal data. You also have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing. To exercise these rights, contact privacy@salzventure.com.

Right to Complain. You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (e.g., the ICO in the UK, or the relevant supervisory authority in your EU member state).

Data Protection Officer. No EU/UK Data Protection Officer has been designated at this time; this will be reviewed as the service grows. For all GDPR/UK GDPR inquiries, contact privacy@salzventure.com.
