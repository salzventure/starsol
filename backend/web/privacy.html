Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 28, 2026 · StarSol

StarSol provides astronomy media, constellation learning, sky guidance, and subscription features for TV and web. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how to contact us.

Information We Collect

  • Account information such as email address, display name, authentication provider, and subscription plan.
  • Device and pairing information needed to connect a TV app to your account.
  • Approximate observing location if you grant location access or save a location, used for sky conditions, ISS pass planning, and aurora context.
  • Usage information such as app screens, playback state, feature interactions, and error logs used to operate and improve the service.
  • Limited billing metadata from Stripe for web purchases and Google Play subscription status for Google TV purchases. We do not receive or store raw payment-card numbers.

How We Use Information

  • To provide account access, subscriptions, restore purchases, and TV pairing.
  • To show location-aware astronomy guidance when you enable those features.
  • To maintain security, debug failures, prevent abuse, and improve product quality.
  • To send transactional messages such as account, password, billing, and service notices.

Location Data

Location is used only for astronomy features such as sky context, ISS pass planning, weather/cloud context, and aurora watch status. You can deny browser or device location permission; StarSol will use a default or saved location until you change it.

Payments

Web payments are processed by Stripe. Google TV subscriptions are processed by Google Play Billing. Each payment provider handles payment data under its own terms and privacy policy. StarSol receives only the information needed to verify entitlement and manage access.

Sharing

We share information with service providers that help operate StarSol, including hosting, email, authentication, analytics, billing, and astronomy/weather data services. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect users, StarSol, or the public.

Retention

We keep account and subscription records while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward for legal, security, accounting, and operational needs. We keep diagnostic logs for a limited period unless needed to investigate abuse or security issues.

Your Choices

  • You can cancel subscriptions through the platform where you subscribed.
  • You can disable location permission in your device or browser settings.
  • You can request account deletion or data access by emailing hello@starsol.app.

Children

StarSol is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests: hello@starsol.app.
