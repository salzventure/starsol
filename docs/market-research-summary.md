## Market

- US TAM: 45–55M highly engaged space/astronomy adults
- US SAM: 8–12M streaming-first, willing-to-pay-for-niche
- Y3 SOM: ~300K paying subs at realistic 2–3% SAM capture
- Space economy: $570B (2023) → $1.8T by 2035 (McKinsey/WEF)
- Tailwind: JWST caused 19x Cloudflare traffic spike; Starship hits 5M+ concurrent on YouTube

## Competitive landscape

| Competitor | Price | Threat | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| NASA+ | Free | Critical | Free, ad-free, all platforms — but no curation, no storytelling, UX problems |
| MagellanTV | ~$6.99/mo | Med-High | Best content vertical but zero brand identity, stale app |
| CuriosityStream | $4.99/mo | Medium | Pivoting to B2B/AI licensing; FY2024 actual $51.1M |
| Nebula | $6/mo | Medium | ~680K subs; creator-driven, not cinematic |
| Disney+/NatGeo | $9.99+/mo | Medium | Owns Cosmos IP but won't build a focused vertical |

**The gap:** No paid competitor focuses on astronomy as a brand. Premium cinematic slot is open.

## Pricing (fact-checked against comps)

- $4.99/mo Explorer or **$39.99/yr** (annual is critical — this audience prepays)
- $7.99/mo Observer ($9.99 is the danger zone — CuriosityStream killed it)
- Free tier essential for funnel
- Institutional $1,500–$5,000/yr (planetariums, K-12, museums)

## Content licensing — what's actually free

- NASA, STScI/JWST, ESO, NOIRLab: all public domain or CC BY 4.0 — VERIFIED
- Year-1 content budget: $500–900K for a credible catalog
- Music licensing requires Artlist Max (~$480/yr+) — standard plans don't cover SVOD

## Platform economics (fact-checked)

- Roku: 20% rev share via Roku Pay
- Apple TV: 30% (15% only if under $1M/yr via Small Biz Program — not automatic)
- Amazon Fire TV: 20% (reduced from 30% in Q4 2021)
- Direct/web via Stripe: ~2.9% — push this channel

## Unit economics

- LTV per sub: ~$156 (at 35% annual churn, $6.99/mo, 65% gross margin)
- Target CAC: <$52 (3:1 LTV:CAC)
- Creator YouTube sponsorships: $8–18 CAC (Anton Petrov, Dr. Becky, Cool Worlds)
- Paid social: $25–60 CAC — last resort

## Build timeline (1–2 devs)

- Roku MVP: 4–6 months
- Full 3-platform (Roku + ATV + Fire): 8–10 months
- Roku certification: 2–6 weeks elapsed (plan for it)

## Risks

1. NASA+ is free — must answer "why pay?" with cinematic storytelling + curation
2. Catalog exhaustion in 60–90 days for heavy users — annual plans + ambient mode + live events = the defense
3. Platform 20–30% rev share — drive direct web signup via Stripe
4. Trademark — USPTO clearance before brand investment (no conflicts found in streaming/entertainment)
5. Music licensing — budget Artlist Max, not standard

## Exit landscape

- No pure astronomy streaming acquisition has ever happened
- Strategic interest at $10–15M ARR / 100K+ subs
- Today's niche SVOD multiple: 1–3x revenue
- Build for cashflow, not exit
