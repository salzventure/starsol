What you'll see

Four windows onto the night.

01

Ambient Mode

Turn your television into an observatory window. Drift through Hubble and JWST imagery at the pace of starlight — no narration, no captions, just the quiet light of distant things.

02

JWST Gallery

Every public James Webb release, in native 4K. Pillars of Creation. Carina. Cosmic Cliffs. Curated, annotated, and updated within hours of each new image drop.

03

Sky Events Calendar

Meteor showers, eclipses, planetary conjunctions, lunar phases. Filtered for your hemisphere, your latitude, your bortle class. Tomorrow night, the Perseids peak at 2:14am.

04

Geolocated Alerts

A push when the ISS passes overhead. An alert when the aurora forecast crosses Kp 6 for your latitude. Notice given in minutes, not hours. Step outside. Look up.
