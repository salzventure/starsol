Catalogued

Eight you can find tonight.

ORION

The Hunter — visible November through March. Betelgeuse, his shoulder, is a dying red supergiant 642 light-years away.

URSA MAJOR

The Great Bear — circumpolar, visible year-round. The Big Dipper's handle traces an arc to Arcturus.

CASSIOPEIA

The Queen — a W against the northern sky, year-round circumpolar. Vain enough to be tied to her throne for eternity.

CYGNUS

The Swan — soaring down the summer Milky Way. Deneb, the tail, anchors the Summer Triangle.

SCORPIUS

The Scorpion — low in the southern summer sky. Antares, its red heart, rivals Mars in brilliance and color.

LEO

The Lion — striding the spring sky. Regulus, the little king, sits almost exactly on the ecliptic.

GEMINI

The Twins — Castor and Pollux, brothers half-mortal, half-divine, fixed forever in winter's eastern sky.

TAURUS

The Bull — Aldebaran his red eye, the Pleiades on his shoulder. The Hyades V opens toward Orion across the winter sky.
